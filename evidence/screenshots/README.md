# Screenshot Evidence

Save screenshots of your own lab results in this folder. Suggested filenames:
- `01-wireshark-telnet-filter.png`
- `02-telnet-follow-tcp-stream-redacted.png`
- `03-wireshark-ssh-filter.png`
- `04-ssh-follow-tcp-stream.png`

Before committing, check that screenshots do not expose passwords, tokens, private keys, public IPs, or personal information. Redact sensitive content first. Do not use screenshots from other people's work as if they were your own.
